  Seam position: Back

AFTER PRINTING
  - Clear the four M3 holes in the back shell with a 3.5 mm drill or a spare screw (strings close them).
  - Heat-set the four M3 x 5.7 brass inserts into the front shell corner posts (pockets are 4.3 mm). Iron at the lowest dial setting (200 on our LDK 60 W), slow, light pressure, until nearly flush, then press flush and let cool.
  - Stylus: slide the flange on the end of the body into the side slot of the knob until the thin neck sits in the narrow slit. The lip inside the knob sits behind the flange, so pulling the knob pulls the stylus. Put a drop of CA glue in the slot so it cannot slide back out.

WHAT CHANGED IN 7 FINAL (vs 6.6)
  - Charger tabs reinforced: 7.4 wide with a round 3.7 mm end behind the screw hole (were 6.0 x 8.4 and snapping). Ring around the hole 2.35 mm each side (was 1.65).
  - Stylus: three thicker bodies (3.82, 3.88, 3.98 mm) to replace the 3.5 mm one, flange nudged down 0.1 mm so it touches the bed.
  - Front shell, back shell, straps and knob are the printed and verified v6.6 parts: speaker posts and switch screw holes were tested and are perfect.
